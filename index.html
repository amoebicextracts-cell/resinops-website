How it works

Operational from day one

STEP 01

Import your existing data

Drop your existing spreadsheets, METRC exports, or lab CSV files. ResinOps automatically identifies what type of data it is — harvest batches, COAs, sales orders, spray logs — and imports it with the right structure. No manual mapping required.

STEP 02

Connect your operation

Configure your rooms, strains, team, and SKUs once. Every batch created after that automatically flows through cultivation → harvest → processing → QC → sales. Data entered in one place shows up everywhere it's relevant.

STEP 03

Run your business from one screen

Your dashboard shows confirmed revenue, upcoming harvests, QC holds, compliance alerts, and strain performance — updated in real time. When an inspector calls, your pesticide log and batch records are one click away.
